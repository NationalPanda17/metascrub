    What is photo metadata?
    
      
    
        
    Location
    Phones write GPS coordinates into every photo by default. Posting the original file can reveal your home.
    
        
    Identity
    Serial numbers and unique IDs can link photos taken by the same device, even across accounts.
    
        
    Time
    Capture times down to the sub-second, often with your time zone offset.
    
        
    Hidden data
    Thumbnails, depth maps and bytes after the image end can hold content you thought you removed.
    
        
    Videos
    Phone videos store GPS, the phone model and the exact recording time, just like photos.
    
        
    Documents
    Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF files carry author names, your company, comment authors, editing time and even photos with GPS.
    
      
    
      
    Cleaning is lossless: metadata is cut out or blanked in place, and pictures, video and document content are never re-compressed. Many social sites strip metadata on upload, but email, cloud links, messaging "send as file" and forums often don't.
